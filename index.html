Fast briefing

What you are really interviewing for

1

Ownership, not just experience

A Team Lead does the work, but also owns the outcome. You need to sound like the person who checks the job before anyone has to ask.

2

Communication under pressure

Control Tower, office updates, start-time problems, access issues, missing photos, deficiencies - if communication breaks, the job breaks.

3

Documentation that protects the job

Before/after photos, clear notes, timestamps, deficiencies, and closeout details prove what happened and what was fixed.

4

Safety and quality standards

If something is unsafe, you correct it. If it cannot be corrected, you stop the operation and communicate upward.

5

Coaching the crew

A Team Lead sets the pace, teaches, keeps people moving, and helps less-experienced technicians get better.
